ATThe Agent's Toolkit

Every listing, every reply, in your voice.

Built solo, 2026 · Paused September 2026

What it was

Software for real estate agents. One place to write listings, social captions and client follow-ups that actually sounded like the agent wrote them, and to track a deal from first contact through to close.

Four months, one person, product and design and code.

What shipped

  • ListingMLS details into full site copy, in a chosen tone and length. Address, price and beds came prefilled from the project.
  • SocialCaptions for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok, grounded in cited research on what performs rather than engagement tricks.
  • Follow-UpDrafted against a real contact from the pipeline instead of a blank name field. Sending one reset that contact's follow-up clock.
  • ReplyRead a rejected offer and answered what was actually said, without inventing false hope or a reason to reconsider.
  • VoiceCalibrated once from real writing samples. Correct it in one place and every other tool carried the fix.
Underneath those: a project and client tracker, and an open-house QR code that turned a scan into a tracked lead before the visitor left the driveway.

Why it's paused

I went looking for who would actually pay for it. Transaction management is a crowded, mature category, and nearly every product in it begins the moment a deal goes under contract.

The stretch before that is genuinely underserved. It is also where the agents who need the most help are the ones with the least money and the highest attrition, and the incumbents were already adding AI on top of the data and distribution they had. I could not find a position I could hold on my own.

So I stopped, rather than finish a product I had stopped believing in. The code still exists. If a real reason to restart turns up, it will.
